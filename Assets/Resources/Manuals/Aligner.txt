<size=150%><b>Mask Aligner Operations</b></size>

The Mask Aligner is used to transfer patterns from a photomask onto your photoresist-coated wafer.

<b>Step 1: Inserting the Mask</b>
Carefully retrieve your photomask from the storage dock and place it face-down onto the upper chuck of the aligner.

[IMAGE: mask_insertion_diagram]

<b>Step 2: Substrate Loading</b>
Ensure the Top Lid is completely removed. Place your coated wafer onto the lower vacuum chuck. The system will automatically secure it.

<b>Step 3: Exposure Parameters</b>
Set the following parameters carefully before running the exposure sequence:
- <b>Dose:</b> Standard range is 25-35 mJ/cm²
- <b>Defocus:</b> Keep at 0.0 unless performing advanced multi-layer alignment
- <b>NA:</b> Numerical Aperture dictates resolution

[IMAGE: exposure_settings_screenshot]

Once parameters are locked, press RUN to begin the UV exposure sequence. Do not open the chamber during exposure!
