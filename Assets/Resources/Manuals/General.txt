<size=150%><b>General Cleanroom Protocol</b></size>

Welcome to the Virtual Fabrication facility. This manual covers the basic operating procedures.

<b>1. Safety First</b>
Always ensure that you are wearing the appropriate protective gear before interacting with any chemical stations. 

[IMAGE: safety_gear_diagram]

<b>2. Wafer Handling</b>
Wafers are extremely delicate. Never pick them up with your bare hands. Always use the designated wafer carriers or vacuum wands when transferring them between machines.

<b>3. Chemical Disposal</b>
Do not pour used developer or DI water back into the main supply. Use the designated chemical disposal sinks located at the end of the workbench.

<size=120%><b>Common Troubleshooting</b></size>
- If the Spin Coater does not start, ensure the vacuum seal is active.
- If the Mask Aligner is jammed, verify that the Top Lid has been removed before attempting to insert a new substrate.
