<size=36><b><u>SC-1 Wafer Cleaning</u></b></size>

1. Approach the cleaning station and click on the leftmost bottle (SC-1 solution) to grab it
2. Click the beaker placed on top of the cleaning hotplate to pour the SC-1 solution, then place the bottle back
3. Click on the highlighted region on the cleaning hotplate's knob to open its menu
4. Start the SC-1 cleaning process and observe the timer
5. Once cleaning is complete, close the UI menu
6. Click on the Distilled Water (DI Water) bottle (2nd bottle) to grab it
7. Click the beaker in front of the DI water bottle to pour the rinse solution, then place the bottle back
8. Click on the cleaned wafer inside the SC-1 beaker to retrieve it
9. Place the wafer into the DI water beaker to rinse it
10. Once rinsed, click on the wafer inside the DI beaker to retrieve it


<size=36><b><u>Dehydration Bake</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: ovenImg]

1. Bring the rinsed wafer over to the Dehydration Oven opposite the cleaning station
2. Click on the oven door to open it and load the wafer inside
3. Click on the highlighted region on the oven's control panel to open its UI menu
4. Adjust temperature and time settings if required, then start the Dehydration Bake
5. Once dehydration is complete, close the menu and click on the wafer inside the oven to retrieve it
6. Bring the dehydrated wafer to the PR Pre-Bake hotplate bench next to the oven and place it down


<size=36><b><u>Spincoater</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: spincoaterImg]

1. Approach the spincoater machine, and use Left mouse click to click the highlighted portion on its right to open up its menu
2. Toggle the spincoater to open its hatch
3. Look over to the hotplate on the left and click the wafer to grab it
4. Place the Wafer on the spincoater by clicking the spincoater region
5. Toggle the spincoater to close its hatch
6. Adjust the RPM and spin time values if required
7. Dispense the Photoresist (PR) solution onto the wafer 
8. Toggle the spincoater and start the spin operation
9. Once complete, Thickness value of the PR will be visible
10. Remove the wafer and bring it over to the hotplate on the left.


<size=36><b><u>Hotplate</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: hotplateImg]  

1. Place the PR coated wafer on top of the Hotplate
2. Click on the highlighted region on the knob to bring up its menu
3. Adjust the hotplate’s set temperature and time if required 
4. Start prebake/softbake 
5. Observe metrics displayed on the ellipsometry visual at the top right of the menu
 
       
<size=36><b><u>Wafer Carrier</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: wafercarrierImg]  

1. Take the wafer from the hotplate
2. Click on the wafer carrier once to open the Wafer Carrier and to store the wafer safely.
3. After it is stored, click it again to move it around the cleanroom
4. Bring the carrier to a storage bench in the next room.
5. Place it on the right side of storage bench


<size=36><b><u>Storage Bench</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: storageImg]

Note: Top lid of the mask tray is interactable on the left of this table, the photomask is available in the middle (First item) and wafer carrier is interactable on the right.


<size=36><b><u>Mask Aligner</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: maskalignerImg]

1. Approach the mask aligner, and click on the highlighted region of the Monitor to interact with its software
2. Click on open recipe
3. Select the default top options in general settings (Man. Top Side, Transparent, Constant dose, Vacuum Contact)
4. Observe the exposure settings down the middle of the screen.
5. Adjust the dose, defocus and Numerical Aperature of the lens if required
6. Click on Run
7. Click on continue to view all intermediate steps until Photomask must be loaded
8. To exit, Click the X button located at the top right

<size=36><b><u>To Insert Photomask</u></b></size>

1. Click on the first Photomask from the Photomask tray on the storage bench
2. Adjust the expected Critical dimension if required
3. Click on preferred pattern to select Photomask
4. Place the photomask on the Chuck of the mask tray by precisely clicking on it
5. Click on the highlighted below the monitor to access a separate menu
6. Toggle the masktray and head back to Mask Aligner software

<size=36><b><u>To Insert Wafer</u></b></size>

1. Toggle the mask tray out
2. Click on the mask tray (not the chuck area) to grab the top lid
3. Keep the toplid on the left side of the storage bench
4. Click on the wafer carrier to grab the wafer
5. place it onto the chuck by clicking on it precisely
6. Toggle back the mask tray and continue the steps in the software
7. Once exposure is complete, The exposed pattern can be observed in the other UI.
8. toggle the mask tray out and place the wafer in the wafer carrier
9. Bring it back to the hotplate bench


<size=36><b><u>Post Exposure Bake</u></b></size>

1. Place the wafer on the hotplate
2. Click on Start Postbake from its UI
3. Observe the post exposure bake pattern on the wafer
4. Bring the wafer to the development station using the wafer carrier
5. The wafer carrier can be placed roughly to the LEFT of both beakers


<size=36><b><u>Development Station</u></b></size>

[IMAGE: developerImg]

1. Click on the big bottle containing the developer solution to grab it
2. Click on the RIGHT beaker to pour the solution, wait for it to complete and place back the bottle in the same place
3. On the LEFT beaker, repeat the same process with Distilled water bottle
4. Place the wafer on the developer solution beaker
5. Click on the highlighted region roughly on the right of the beakers to access the UI
6. Adjust the time duration in which the wafer is immersed in the liquid
7. Click on "Swerve Dev Beaker" to shake and swerve the liquid along the wafer
8. Once the swerve time is complete, bring the wafer to LEFT beaker and repeat with "Swerve DI Beaker"
9. The pattern will be visible after dip in Developer solution, with its final pattern after development
